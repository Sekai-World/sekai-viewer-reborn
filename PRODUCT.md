# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Sekai Viewer serves casual Project Sekai players, wiki and database maintainers, pro players, data miners, and game tool developers. They use it to browse current and historical game data, inspect cards, songs, events, regions, metadata, and assets, and cross-check information across localized servers.

## Product Purpose

The product is a multi-site viewer platform for Project Sekai data and related tools. The content site should make game data fast to find, easy to filter, and reliable enough for reference work. Success means users can move from broad browsing to specific records without friction, while preserving enough detail for power users and maintainers.

## Brand Personality

Fan-site playful, precise, and helpful. The interface can carry the energy of the game community through icons, character and unit imagery, and light motion, but the core browsing experience should stay readable, organized, and task-focused.

## Anti-references

Avoid bland enterprise dashboards, heavy marketing landing pages, generic SaaS decoration, overly dark "developer terminal" styling, and game-fan clutter that makes reference data hard to scan. Avoid motion that slows low-end devices or blocks users who prefer reduced motion.

## Design Principles

- Make browsing feel playful without sacrificing data density.
- Prefer recognizable game and unit signals over generic decoration.
- Keep filters, sort controls, and list views fast for repeated use.
- Preserve regional and localization context wherever labels or assets can differ.
- Treat performance and accessibility as part of the product experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA. Support reduced motion both for accessibility and low-end devices. Prioritize keyboard access, visible focus states, readable contrast in every theme, non-color-only state indicators, and responsive layouts that remain usable on mobile.
