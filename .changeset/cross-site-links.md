The content site sidebar gains a "More sites" group that links to the Story Reader, Live2D, and Chibi on the media lab site and to the Event Tracker and Music Recommender on the tools site. Event, card, and character pages link to their stories in the Story Reader. Each link appears only when its site's address is configured. Shared sidebar links can now be marked as links to another site, which opens them in a new window with an indicator icon.
