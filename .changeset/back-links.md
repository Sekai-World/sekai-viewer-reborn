The tools site and the media lab site link back to the content site. Both sidebars gain a "More sites" group with a "Sekai Viewer" link, the Story Reader links to the event, card, or character a story belongs to, and the Event Tracker links to the tracked event. Each link appears only when the content site's address is configured. The 0.2.0 update log mentions the new links.
